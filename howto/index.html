1

Get your marriage license

In Georgia, marriage licenses come from the county Probate Court. Good news: there's no waiting period and no blood test.

$56Lowndes license fee
$16With a premarital education certificate
0 daysLegal waiting period

Which county can you use?

  • If at least one of you lives in Georgia: you can get your license from any Georgia county's probate court, and it's good for a ceremony anywhere in Georgia.
  • If neither of you lives in Georgia (e.g., you're coming up from Florida): get the license in the county where the ceremony will happen.
  • A Georgia license only works for a ceremony held in Georgia.

You can start the application online at lcprobate.lowndescounty.com, but you both still need to show up in person to sign, pay, and show ID.

What to bring

  • Both of you, in person.
  • Photo ID showing your current legal name: driver's license, passport, or military ID (or a certified birth certificate plus a photo ID).
  • Married before? A certified copy with a raised seal of your most recent divorce decree or your late spouse's death certificate. Lowndes makes no exceptions here.
  • Originals or certified copies only. No photocopies, phone pictures, or digital IDs.
  • Payment: cash (exact change, or no bills over $20), Visa, MasterCard, Discover, money order, or cashier's check.
  • Your notarized premarital education certificate, if you have one (see Step 2).
Changing your last name? Decide before you apply. In Georgia you write your new last name on the marriage license application, and that becomes your legal proof of the new name.
There's no legal waiting period, but Lowndes recommends getting your license 5 to 7 days before the wedding. Don't count on a same-day rush on a Friday afternoon.

2

Save $40 with premarital education

Georgia knocks $40 off your license if you complete a qualifying premarital program. In Lowndes that brings it from $56 to $16. To count:

  • You took it together, within the 12 months before you apply.
  • It was at least 6 hours on marriage topics.
  • It was taught by a Georgia-licensed counselor, social worker, marriage & family therapist, psychologist, or psychiatrist, or by clergy (or someone they trained).
  • You bring the original, notarized Certificate of Completion. You can get the blank form from the Probate Court.
3

Choose your officiant

The Lowndes County Probate Court no longer performs wedding ceremonies, so you'll need your own officiant. Georgia law (O.C.G.A. § 19-3-30) allows judges, magistrates, and ministers or other people authorized by a religious society to perform ceremonies.

Your officiant also handles the legal side: signing the license and returning it to the Probate Court within 30 days. A good one makes sure that actually happens.

4

Have the ceremony and return the license

  • Hand your marriage license to your officiant before the ceremony.
  • After the ceremony, the officiant completes the certificate of marriage.
  • The officiant must return the license to the Probate Court that issued it within 30 days of the wedding.
  • Once it's recorded, you can order certified copies.
5

Order certified copies

You'll need a certified copy (not a photocopy) for almost every name change and insurance update. Order 2 or 3 so you can work on several at once.

Lowndes: $10 per certified copy. Request in person, by mail, or online. Only the couple can request copies. Mail requests need a photocopy of your driver's license or state ID and a money order or cashier's check.

6

Change your name (in this order)

Keeping your name? Skip this step. There's no deadline to change your name after marriage. If you are changing it, the order matters:

① Social Security first

  • It's free. Some couples can do it online through a my Social Security account. Otherwise, fill out Form SS-5 and go in person or mail it.
  • SSA suggests waiting at least 30 days after the wedding before applying online.
  • Bring a certified marriage certificate and your ID.

② Georgia DDS: driver's license or state ID

  • Georgia requires a new license showing your new name within 60 days of the name change.
  • Go to a DDS Customer Service Center in person with your certified marriage certificate, your current license, and proof that SSA has updated your record.
  • Wait a day or two after updating SSA so DDS's system can see the change.

③ Everything else

  • Passport
  • Voter registration
  • Bank accounts, credit cards, and loans
  • Employer / payroll (HR needs your new name for your W-2)
  • Vehicle title & registration (do this after your license)
  • Lease or mortgage, utilities, phone
  • Doctors, pharmacy, and insurance cards
7

Update your insurance

Getting married opens a short window to change your health coverage outside open enrollment. Don't let it close.

30 daysTypical window to add a spouse to an employer plan. Ask HR right away.
60 daysMarketplace (HealthCare.gov) special enrollment window after the wedding

Health insurance

  • Marketplace: you get 60 days from the wedding date to enroll or switch plans. At least one of you must have had qualifying coverage for at least 1 day in the 60 days before the wedding.
  • Marketplace coverage usually starts the first of the following month.
  • Job-based plans often give only 30 days. Check with HR before the wedding.

Also worth updating

  • Car insurance: combining policies often earns a multi-car or married discount.
  • Renters / homeowners: add your spouse and combine policies if you're moving in together.
  • Life insurance & retirement accounts: update beneficiaries.
  • Taxes: next filing season you can file jointly. Update your W-4 at work.

Your timeline at a glance

3 to 12 months before: Book your officiant. Optional: finish premarital education (within 12 months of applying).
1 to 2 weeks before: Get your marriage license together at the Probate Court. Lowndes suggests 5 to 7 days ahead.
Wedding day: Give the license to your officiant and say "I do."
Within 30 days: Your officiant returns the signed license. Add your spouse to an employer health plan.
After it's recorded: Order 2 or 3 certified copies.
30+ days after: Update your name with Social Security.
Within 60 days of the name change: New Georgia driver's license. Marketplace health changes are also due within 60 days of the wedding.

Quick questions

Can we get married the same day we get our license?

Legally, yes. Georgia has no waiting period. Just give yourself a buffer in case of a missing document.

Can the judge at the courthouse marry us?

The Lowndes County Probate Court no longer performs ceremonies. You'll need an officiant, and Azalea Vows can help.

We live in Florida. Can we marry in Valdosta?

Yes. If neither of you lives in Georgia, get your license from the probate court in the county where the ceremony will take place.

Do we need witnesses?

Not in Lowndes County. The license doesn't need witness signatures. You're welcome to have them for the moment, though!
